Account Deletion Policy

Developer: Omar Nasser · Contact: support@leap-arena.com

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Leap Arena app
  2. Go to your Profile
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted

What Gets Deleted

All of your personal data is removed, including your name, email address, fitness scores, and leaderboard data.

  • Name and account details
  • Email address
  • Fitness scores and trial history
  • Leaderboard data and rankings

Retention Period

Your data is immediately and permanently deleted upon confirmation. Once your account deletion is confirmed, this action cannot be undone and your data cannot be recovered.

Need Help?

If you run into any issues deleting your account, or have questions about this policy, reach out and we'll help.
