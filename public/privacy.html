Privacy Policy

Effective: June 8, 2026 · Last Updated: October 29, 2026 · Developer: Omar Nasser

1. Introduction

Welcome to Leap Arena. This Privacy Policy explains how Omar Nasser ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use the Leap Arena mobile application available on iOS and web platforms.

By creating an account and using Leap Arena, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Full name (first and last)
  • Display name / username
  • Email address
  • Gender
  • Country of residence
  • Invite code used at registration
  • Password (stored encrypted — we never see it in plain text)
  • Instagram handle (optional, provided for invite code request review)

2.2 Fitness and Performance Data

  • Strength tier and power tier rankings
  • Trial completion times and personal bests
  • Assessment results (pull-ups, dips, push-ups, muscle-ups)
  • Static world, Power world, and Endurance (1MM) scores
  • Weekly challenge scores
  • Trial history (attempts, completions, abandoned sessions)
  • Workout programs created, assigned, or completed through the Coaching Center
  • Exercise library interactions and saved custom exercises
  • Coaching relationship data (your assigned coach ID and warrior ID if you participate in the Coaching Center)
  • Program block completion records within assigned warrior programs

2.3 Device and Technical Data

  • Device timezone (collected automatically at registration)
  • Push notification token (for sending workout reminders)
  • Platform type (iOS or web)
  • App session data
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Device identifier (associated with crash and diagnostic reports)

2.4 AI Coach Conversations

If you use the AI Coach, we collect:

  • The messages you send to the AI Coach and the replies it gives you
  • What the AI Coach did to answer you, for example which of your records it read (such as your tier, assessment results and workout logs) and which program changes it proposed
  • Technical details of each reply, such as when it was sent, how long it took and whether an error happened
  • Replies you report from inside the App, and replies our automatic safety checks mark

Your messages may include information about your health, such as injuries, pain or body weight, if you choose to share it. Only share what you are comfortable with.

2.5 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise GPS location
  • We do not collect health data from Apple Health or Google Fit
  • We do not collect camera or microphone data
  • We do not serve advertisements or collect advertising identifiers
  • We do not use tracking pixels or behavioral advertising
  • We do not access your camera. We request photo library access only to save workout completion banners to your device

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Creating and managing your Leap Arena account
  • Authenticating your identity when you sign in
  • Calculating and displaying your strength tier, power tier, and overall rankings
  • Displaying your performance on leaderboards
  • Sending push notifications for workout reminders and arena updates
  • Processing password reset requests via email
  • Preventing cheating and validating trial submission times on our servers
  • Improving the App and fixing bugs
  • Delivering personalised workout programs through the Coaching Center
  • Tracking your progress within assigned warrior programs
  • Answering you in the AI Coach and building or changing your program when you ask it to
  • Reviewing AI Coach conversations to find mistakes, poor advice or inappropriate replies, and improving the AI Coach so they do not happen again
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

4.1 Supabase (Database and Authentication)

Your account data and fitness records are stored securely on Supabase. For more information, visit supabase.com/privacy.

4.2 Apple (iOS Platform)

Push notifications are delivered via Apple's Push Notification service (APNs). For more information, visit apple.com/privacy.

4.3 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

If you purchase a Leap Arena membership subscription, RevenueCat processes your subscription and purchase data (including your account identifier and subscription status) on our behalf to manage entitlements and renewals. RevenueCat does not receive your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. For more information, visit revenuecat.com/privacy.

4.4 Sentry (Crash and Performance Monitoring)

We use Sentry to collect crash reports, performance diagnostics, and an associated device identifier, which helps us identify and fix bugs. For more information, visit sentry.io/privacy.

4.5 Anthropic (AI Coach)

The AI Coach is powered by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. When you use the AI Coach, your messages and the training information it needs to answer you (such as your tier, program and workout logs) are sent to Anthropic to generate the reply. Your email address and payment details are never sent. For more information, visit anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Authentication tokens are stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain)
  • Server-side validation prevents submission of fraudulent fitness scores
  • Access to the database is restricted to authenticated users only

6. Account Deletion and Data Retention

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. You can do this directly within the App by navigating to Profile → Delete Account. Upon deletion, all your personal information, fitness data, tier rankings, and trial history are permanently and immediately erased from our servers and cannot be recovered.

AI Coach conversations. We keep your AI Coach conversations on our servers for 90 days, then delete them. If we are reviewing a conversation to fix a problem with the AI Coach, we keep it until that review is finished. Conversations are read only by the developer, Omar Nasser, and only to check and improve the AI Coach. Clearing the chat in the App removes it from your device but not from our servers; deleting your account removes it from both.

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. Upon requesting account deletion, your personal data is permanently erased immediately. Some anonymised, aggregated statistical data may be retained indefinitely and cannot be used to identify you personally.

  • Right to Deletion: Delete your account directly in the App under Profile → Delete Account

7.2 Additional Rights for EEA / UK Users (GDPR)

  • Right to data portability
  • Right to restriction of processing

  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

8. Children's Privacy

Leap Arena is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Push Notifications

We collect your device's push notification token to send workout reminders and arena event announcements. You can disable push notifications at any time through your iOS device settings under Settings → Notifications → Leap Arena.

10. Leaderboards and Public Data

Leap Arena features leaderboards displaying user performance rankings. Your display name, rank and scores are visible to other signed-in users, together with your country (shown as a flag or country code). Leaderboards can be filtered by gender, which means a filtered list shows the gender of the people in it. Your email address and full name are never displayed.

Users can share an image of a leaderboard outside the App (for example on social media or in messaging apps). A shared leaderboard image shows the display name, rank and score of the top three athletes and of the person sharing it. Shared images never include country, gender, email address or full name.

You can hide yourself from leaderboards at any time in Settings → Leaderboards. While hidden, other users do not see you on any leaderboard (Strength, the worlds, Well-Rounded, the Weekly Challenge or the Journey community board) and you are left out of their rankings; your results still count for you, you still see your own rank, and Team Challenge boards still show you to your own teammates. You can show yourself again at any time.

Fair play. To keep leaderboards fair, results far beyond what is realistic (for example, above recorded world-class levels or a sudden very large jump over your own best) are automatically marked for review by our team. Being marked changes nothing on its own. After a review we may dismiss the mark, hide you from one area's leaderboards or from all of them, or, for repeated or clear abuse, remove you from leaderboards permanently. A hold hides you from other users only; your results still count for you and the rest of the App works as normal. In the App you can see which results were reviewed, accept a reset (only those results are removed; your other results and your strength tier stay) or send us a note asking us to look again. We keep a record of reviews, decisions and notes for as long as your account exists.

If you have not used the App for 45 days or more, we may hide you from leaderboards until you return. Nothing is removed, and you can show yourself again with one tap when you come back.
