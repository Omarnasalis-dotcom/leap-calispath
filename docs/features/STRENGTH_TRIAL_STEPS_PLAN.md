# Strength Trial Steps: plan

Status: **content and rules approved by the owner (2026-10-15)**. Building in small steps.

## The problem

Some tiers add a movement that takes weeks to earn: Hoplite → Spartan needs strict pull-ups and a banded muscle-up, Lochagos → Strategos needs a strict muscle-up, Olympian → Demigod adds weight. An athlete can sit in one tier for a month with nothing to show for it, and that is where people quit.

## The idea (owner decisions, 2026-10-15)

A trial is the exam to **leave** a tier (a Spartan takes the Spartan trial to become a Lochagos). Each step bridges from the trial the athlete already passed to the one ahead: **Neos** athletes get **1 step**, **Ephebe to Eternity** athletes get **2 steps**, Helot athletes none (their first trial is the Helot trial). Fixed 2026-10-15: steps are keyed by the athlete's tier. Steps are **trials**, not training: same trial screen, same For Time format, same "finish every movement" rule. They give small wins between rank-ups.

| Step | What it is |
|---|---|
| **1 · First taste** | Opens with a small dose of the next tier's new movement, then the workout |
| **2 · Bridge** | Opens with a bigger dose of it, then the same workout as Step 1 |

- **The new movement is always the first movement**, while the athlete is fresh.
- **The rest of the workout**: base trials 0–3 (Helot–Hoplite) use that trial **+2 reps on every movement** (on a combined round, +2 on each part). Base trials 4–8 are used **as is**, so no step gets harder than the trial ahead.
- Step 2's workout is the same as Step 1's; only the opening grows.
- **Minimum time** (anti-cheat) = the base trial's minimum, for both steps.
- Below, each section is named by the athletes who see it and the trial it's built from (the "base" trial).

## Rules (owner decisions, 2026-10-15)

1. **In order:** Step 2 unlocks after Step 1.
2. **Journey checkpoint:** the Strength Trial checkpoint (every 2 weeks, odd weeks, unchanged) shows the athlete's **next item on the climb**: this tier's Step 1 → Step 2 → the next tier's trial → that tier's Step 1 … (Helot: Step 1 → Neos trial).
3. **Points only from the Journey checkpoint:** **100 points** for a step, the same as a trial. Steps or trials done from the Strength tab count as cleared but pay nothing; the checkpoint then moves on to the next uncleared item, so nothing pays twice.
4. **Skipped or not finished:** the same item comes back at the next checkpoint.
5. **Never blocks the trial:** the Strength tab keeps the steps and the next tier's trial open at any time.
6. **Never changes tier, never on a leaderboard.** Each step keeps the athlete's own best time and can be replayed.
7. **Pass = every movement finished** (same as trials), at or above the tier's minimum time.
8. **Kept forever:** cleared steps stay cleared; after a rank-up, the next tier's steps unlock.
9. A short "STEP CLEARED" card, and pips on the current tier card in Strength.
10. Movement names stay in English in the Arabic app, like the trials.
11. **Switch at the checkpoint (2026-10-16):** the open Journey trial card has a small switch button (like Switch day). The athlete picks Step 1, Step 2 or the trial; Step 1 and the trial are always open, Step 2 opens once Step 1 is cleared. Any pick finished there closes the checkpoint and pays the 100, a replay of a cleared step included. The pick is remembered for that checkpoint only.

## How it would be built

Small steps, each tested before the next, same as the achievements.

0. **Content:** the steps above as data next to the trials (`src/lib/trialSteps.ts`), built from `RITES_OF_PASSAGE` so the "as is" tiers can't drift from their trials. Tests check: every step opens with its new movement, tiers 0–3 are exactly +2, tiers 4–8 match their trial.
1. **Database:** a `strength_step_attempts` table (every finished step: athlete, tier, step, time) that clients can only read, plus one function, `submit_strength_step`. It accepts only the athlete's current tier, steps in order, and a time at or above the tier's minimum. Steps never touch `trial_history`, so the strength leaderboards stay clean.
1b. **Journey payout:** the `w{N}_trial` slot pays 100 when it's backed by a real result since the program started: a completed trial **or** a finished step (today it counts trials only). The lane picks the checkpoint's item from the athlete's tier and cleared steps.
2. **Trial screen:** a new `step` mode next to progression / practice / eternal. Same runner and timer; no rank-up and no tier change.
3. **Journey checkpoint + Strength card:** the trial checkpoint shows the next item (STEP 1 / STEP 2 / TRIAL); 2 pips on the current tier (1 for Helot) and a START STEP n button. The next tier's trial button stays.
4. **Step cleared card:** short and light, within the animation rules (one clock, transform/opacity only).
5. **Checks:** English and Arabic, light and dark, small phone, tests, type check.

Then an app build. The database part goes live first and is harmless to older apps.
