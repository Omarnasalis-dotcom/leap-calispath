## 5. Tone

The app's English is short and energetic ("KEEP FIGHTING", "LEAP NOW"). In Arabic these become short commands in MSA, for example:

| English | Arabic |
|---|---|
| Keep fighting | واصل القتال |
| Leap now | انطلق الآن |
| Grand finale | الجولة الختامية |
| Play again | العب مرة أخرى |

Men and women: Arabic commands are grammatically masculine by default (ابدأ، سجّل). This is standard in apps, and the list uses it throughout. Tell me if you would rather address women users in the feminine form based on their profile gender (more work, more natural).
