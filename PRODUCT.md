# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Serious calisthenics / street-workout athletes tracking progression through a tiered strength system. They open the app to check their current rank, log or start a trial, see how close they are to the next tier, and see where they stand on the leaderboard against other warriors. The app functions as both a training log and a competitive ladder — used quickly, often around a workout, so at-a-glance legibility of rank/progress/next-action matters more than exploratory browsing.

## Product Purpose

A gamified calisthenics progression tracker built around a 9-tier Spartan-ranked strength system (Helot → Neos → Ephebe → Hoplite → Spartan → Lochagos → Strategos → Olympian → Demigod → Eternity), plus parallel Power, Static, and 1-Minute-Max disciplines, trials, and leaderboards, with coaching features for coaches/admins. Success looks like a user who keeps coming back to chase the next tier and check their rank — the app should make progression feel earned and status feel real.

## Brand Personality

Premium, disciplined, precise — with the Spartan/warrior theme intact underneath. The goal is not to soften the warrior framing, but to execute it with more restraint and craft: intensity expressed through precision (tight hierarchy, considered spacing, disciplined use of the red accent) rather than through loudness. Think "serious training tool with a warrior ethos," not "war game."

## Anti-references

Must not read as a generic template SaaS/fitness app: gradient hero stat cards, identical repeated stat tiles, cookie-cutter card grids, or any pattern that feels like default React Native boilerplate rather than a considered, branded interface. The existing dark "Stealth Theme" (matte black background, red accent) and Spartan tier system are the identity to preserve and sharpen — not to replace with something generic.

## Design Principles

- **Precision over spectacle** — Spartan intensity is expressed through restraint and craft (tight hierarchy, considered spacing, deliberate color use), not through loud or aggressive visual noise.
- **Earn the drama** — reserve bold color, motion, and emphasis for moments that matter (tier-ups, personal records, unlocks), not as default decoration on every screen.
- **Always-legible training loop** — identity (who am I, what tier), progression (how close to next), and competition (where do I rank) should be scannable at a glance on every core screen.
- **Reject generic template tells** — no default card grids, gradient hero metrics, or cookie-cutter fitness-app patterns; every screen should feel specific to this app, not templated.
- **Respect the athlete's context** — dark-mode-first for gym/low-light use, minimal friction mid-workout, quick scans over exploratory browsing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard mobile baseline: WCAG AA-ish contrast ratios, adequately sized tap targets, and respect for reduced-motion preferences. No additional specific accessibility requirements beyond this baseline at this time.
